Brand kit

Report once. Understand together. Act as a community.

FixMyCity helps neighbours report local problems, confirm each other's reports and see recurring patterns. This kit keeps every screenshot, video and post looking and sounding like one calm, honest, neighbourly product.

Tagline: Your neighbourhood. Your voice. Your impact.

05

Illustration style

One illustration per report category, plus the home page hero. They appear on the home page, the report form and social cards.

Style guide

  • Flat, warm illustration with soft shadows and simple shapes.
  • Palette: teal, cream, terracotta and leaf green, matching the colour palette above.
  • No text, signs with words, logos or number plates inside the image.
  • Show the problem, keep faces small and generic, never a real person.
  • Square 800 x 800 for categories, 16:9 for the hero. Export as WebP.

How they were made

All illustrations were generated with ChatGPT (AI-generated) from written prompts, then resized and converted to WebP. Disclose this wherever they are published. Always give them descriptive alt text.

06

Voice and tone

We sound like a helpful neighbour who keeps notes, not a press office and not an angry commenter.

Neighbourly

Plain words, "we" and "your street". No jargon like "stakeholders" or "grievance redressal".

Honest

It is a prototype and demo data is labelled. We never claim a fix, official action or government link that has not happened.

Hopeful, not heroic

Small actions add up. Celebrate neighbours, never shame officials or individuals.

Say

  • "Report once. Understand together."
  • "Possible recurring issue. Here is why we grouped these."
  • "Looks resolved? Tell your neighbours."
  • "Community confirmed. Awaiting acknowledgement."

Avoid

  • "Government is failing you."
  • "We will get this fixed." (we can't promise that)
  • Naming or blaming private individuals.
  • Telling people to repair hazards themselves.

Safety line to reuse: "Circles are for safe, voluntary activities only. Report hazards on the map instead."
